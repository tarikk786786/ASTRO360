# Technical SEO & Growth Engine Standards

## Standards & Policies
1. **Google Search Essentials Compliance**:
   - Zero spam backlinks, zero mass automated link submission, zero thin AI content farms.
   - Useful tools, accurate astronomical calculations, and people-first educational guides.
2. **Schema.org Knowledge Graph**:
   - `SoftwareApplication` (Price: $0.00 Free launch offer, Category: LifestyleApplication, AggregateRating).
   - `Organization` & `Person` founder relationship linking.
   - `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `Article` structured data.
3. **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) & AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)**:
   - Provide 40-word concise, definitive answers at the top of educational topic clusters to maximize Perplexity, SearchGPT, and Google AI Overview citations.
   - Ground every claim with classical scripture citations (BPHS, Tetrabiblos, Phaladeepika).
4. **Core Web Vitals**:
   - Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) < 2.5s.
   - Interaction to Next Paint (INP) < 200ms.
   - Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) < 0.1.
