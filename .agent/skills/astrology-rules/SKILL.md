# Astrology Rules & Explainability Standards

## Overview
ASTRO360 translates calculated planetary coordinates into interpretative and remedial insights strictly grounded in verified classical treatises and established astrological frameworks.

## Supported Classical Traditions & Source Texts
1. **Parashari Vedic Jyotish**:
   - Primary Source: *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (BPHS)*, *Phaladeepika*, *Saravali*, *Brihat Jataka*.
   - Rules: 12 Rasis, 27 Nakshatras (4 Padas each), 12 Bhavas, Karakatwas, Dasha timelines, Shadbala strength.
2. **Jaimini System**:
   - Primary Source: *Jaimini Upadesha Sutras*.
   - Rules: Chara Karakas (Atmakaraka, Amatyakaraka, etc.), Arudha Padas, Chara Dasha, Rasi Drishti.
3. **KP (Krishnamurti Padhdhati)**:
   - Primary Source: *K.S. Krishnamurti Readers I–VI*.
   - Rules: Placidus house cusps, Nakshatra Star Lord, Sub-Lord, and Sub-Sub Lord ruling signifies.
4. **Western Tropical & Psychological Astrology**:
   - Primary Source: *Ptolemy's Tetrabiblos*, *Dane Rudhyar*, *Liz Greene*, *Robert Hand*.
   - Rules: Major geometric aspects (Conjunction, Trine, Sextile, Square, Opposition), Major Transits, Secondary Progressions, Solar Return.
5. **Traditional Islamic Astronomy (Ilm al-Falak)**:
   - Primary Source: *Al-Biruni's Book of Instruction in the Elements of the Art of Astrology*, *Nasir al-Din al-Tusi*.
   - Rules: Mansions of the Moon (Manazil al-Qamar), Lots (Parts), Planetary Dignities, Qibla alignment.
6. **Chinese BaZi (Four Pillars)**:
   - Primary Source: *San Ming Tong Hui*, *Di Tian Sui*.
   - Rules: Heavenly Stems, Earthly Branches, 5 Elements (Wu Xing), Day Master strength, 10 Gods (Shi Shen).

## Explainability Standards
- Every prediction or synthesis card must provide an explainable **"Why?"** breakdown.
- Display the exact planetary longitude, sign, nakshatra, house, and classical text reference.
- Maintain separate, unblended rule outputs when displaying comparative tradition perspectives.
