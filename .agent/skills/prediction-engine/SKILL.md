# Prediction Engine & Temporal Forecasting Standard

## Overview
The ASTRO360 Prediction Engine synthesizes transit cycles, directional dasha systems, and aspect configurations to calculate structured time-horizon forecasts across key life domains.

## Temporal Calculation Matrix
1. **Time Horizon Granularities**:
   - Daily (Panchanga, Moon transits, Hourly Hora rulers).
   - Weekly (Planetary ingresses, fast-moving aspects).
   - Monthly (Solar ingress, Lunar phases, retrograde stations).
   - Annual (Solar Return, Transits of Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu-Ketu).
   - Multi-Year (Vimshottari Mahadasha/Antardasha, Saturn Sade Sati 7.5-year cycle, Secondary Progressions).

2. **Core Life Dimensions**:
   - **Career & Purpose** (10th house, Sun, Saturn, D10 Dashamsha).
   - **Wealth & Finance** (2nd/11th houses, Jupiter, Dhana Yogas).
   - **Love & Relationships** (7th house, Venus, Jupiter, D9 Navamsa, Synastry aspects).
   - **Vitality & Health** (1st/6th houses, Mars, Sun, Biorhythm cycles).
   - **Spiritual Evolution** (9th/12th houses, Ketu, Jupiter, Atmakaraka).

3. **Convergence & Confidence Quantification**:
   - Each forecast event calculates a **Confidence Score (0–100%)** based on the number of convergent astrological factors (e.g., Active Mahadasha Lord triggering the same house as a major Saturn transit).
   - Transparently note conflicting indications rather than suppressing them.
