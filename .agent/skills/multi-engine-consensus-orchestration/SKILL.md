# Multi-Engine Consensus Orchestration Skill

This skill governs the mathematical aggregation, cross-tradition agreement scoring, and uncertainty quantification across all 7 traditional calculation engines in ASTRO360.

1. **Vedic / Parashari Jyotish (BPHS Ch. 45)**: Vimshottari Mahadasha, Ashtakavarga, Rashi Drishti, and Gochara transit angles.
2. **KP Stellar System (Prof. Krishnamurti Readers I-VI)**: 249 cuspal sub-divisions, sub-lord significations, and Placidus cusps.

4. **Jaimini Sutras (Maharishi Jaimini)**: Chara Dasha sign periods, 7 Karakas (Atmakaraka, Amatyakaraka, Darakaraka), and Arudha Lagna (AL).
5. **Chinese BaZi (San Ming Tong Hui & Di Tian Sui)**: 4 Pillars of Destiny, 10-Year Luck Pillars, and Five Elements (Wu Xing) Day Master balance.
6. **Islamic Ilm al-Falak (Al-Biruni Kitab al-Tafhim 1029 CE)**: 28 Lunar Mansions (Manazil al-Qamar), planetary hours, and Arabic Parts.
7. **Mayan & Mesoamerican (Dresden Codex)**: Tzolk'in 260-day Kin cycles and solar zenith alignments.

## 2. Mathematical Agreement & Confidence Formula

Let $S_i \in [0, 1]$ represent the normalized sentiment/direction of engine $i \in \{1, \dots, N\}$, and let $w_i$ be the historical precision weight of engine $i$:

$$\text{Consensus Score} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^N w_i S_i}{\sum_{i=1}^N w_i} \times 100\%$$

$$\text{Variance } \sigma^2 = \sum_{i=1}^N w_i \left( S_i - \bar{S} \right)^2$$

- **Grade A+ (Strong Consensus)**: Agreement $\ge 90\%$, Variance $\sigma^2 \le 0.05$.
- **Grade A (Moderate Consensus)**: Agreement $\ge 80\%$, Variance $\sigma^2 \le 0.10$.
- **Grade B (System Specific Divergence)**: Agreement $< 80\%$; triggers explicit divergence explanation notes for the seeker.
