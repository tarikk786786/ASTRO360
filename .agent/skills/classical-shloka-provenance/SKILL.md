# Classical Shloka Provenance Standards

## 1. Classical Tradition Scripture Citation Standards
1. **Vedic / Parashari**: *Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra* (BPHS), *Phaladeepika*, *Saravali*, *Brihat Jataka*, *Jataka Parijata*.
2. **Jaimini Sutras**: *Jaimini Upadesha Sutras* (Chara Dasha, Arudha Padas, Atmakaraka).
3. **Western / Hellenistic**: Claudius Ptolemy's *Tetrabiblos*, Vettius Valens' *Anthology*, Dorotheus of Sidon's *Carmen Astrologicum*.
4. **Persian & Medieval Islamic**: Abu Ma'shar's *Kitab al-Madkhal al-Kabir*, Al-Biruni's *Kitab al-Tafhim*.
5. **Chinese BaZi**: *San Ming Tong Hui* (三命通會), *Di Tian Sui* (滴天髓), *Yuan Hai Zi Ping* (淵海子平).

## 2. Provenance Tier System
- **Tier 1**: Direct Canonical Root Scripture with chapter and verse citation.
- **Tier 2**: Classical Commentary from celebrated ancient lineage masters.
- **Tier 3**: Modern Standard Classical Synthesis (e.g. B.V. Raman, K.S. Krishnamurti).
- **Tier 4**: Empirical Statistical / Backtested Observation.
- **Tier 5**: General Archetypal Synthesis.
